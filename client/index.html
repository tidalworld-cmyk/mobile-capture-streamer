🔌 USB-C Video Capture Card Setup

  1. Plug your HDMI video source (camera, game console, laptop) into the capture card HDMI In.
  2. Connect the Type-C side to your phone/PC (use an OTG adapter if needed on Android).
  3. Tap "Capture" in the bottom dock and select your video capture card device.
  4. Select the audio input corresponding to your capture card for HDMI audio.
  5. Enter your YouTube Stream Key in "RTMP" and tap "GO LIVE"!
